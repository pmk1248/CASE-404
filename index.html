📁 CASE FILES — Case 404: The Silence of Blackwood Manor

THE VICTIM

Lord Edmund Blackwood (Age 62, Industrialist, Status: Dead)

Lord Blackwood was wealthy, powerful, and feared. Twenty-five years earlier, a factory worker named Thomas Vale died in a supposed accident at Blackwood Industrial Works. Tonight, Lord Blackwood planned to reveal the truth. He never got the chance.

Cause of Death

Poisoning. The poison: Aconitine.

THE SECRET

A partially burned letter was found in the fireplace. Only one sentence could be read: “You took everything from me.”

Another document revealed that Lord Blackwood was planning to change his will. His nephew, Julian, would inherit nothing. Someone else would receive everything.

🔍 EVIDENCE — Case 404: The Silence of Blackwood Manor

01 — THE POISONED DRINK 🥃

Aconitine was found in Lord Blackwood's brandy glass. But the bottle itself was clean. The poison was added after the drink was poured.

02 — THE SECOND GLASS 🍵

An empty glass was found nearby. It contained traces of sugar and lavender. Only one suspect was known to regularly use lavender sugar.

03 — THE BROKEN WATCH ⌚

Lord Blackwood's pocket watch was found beneath his desk. It stopped at 11:47 PM because it struck the floor during a struggle.

04 — THE CLEAN KEY 🔑

The study door was locked from the inside. The key was found on the floor, but it was completely clean despite the dusty floor.

05 — THE FOOTPRINT 👣

A footprint was found outside the study window, but heavy rain had been falling for hours. The footprint was completely dry—it was planted.

06 — THE COAT BUTTON 🔘

A button matching Julian's coat was found beneath the desk, but the attached thread was dry. Was Julian inside, or was someone framing him?

THE SECRET

🔍 EVIDENCE — Case 405: The Heir Who Never Died

01 — THE MURDER WEAPON 🔪

02 — THE TORN PHOTOGRAPH 🖼️

03 — THE BLACK GLOVE 🧤

A black glove with a small gold thread caught inside was found beside Henry's body.

The archive door was locked, but Henry knew all three keyholders. He opened the door willingly.
