CASE 404

THE SILENCE OF BLACKWOOD MANOR

17 OCTOBER 1894 • RAVENSHIRE

At 11:43 PM, the lights went out.
Fifteen minutes later, they returned.
Lord Edmund Blackwood was dead.


He was found inside his locked study.
The door was locked.
The window was locked.


Four people were inside the manor.
Earlier that evening, Lord Blackwood had made one announcement:


“At midnight, I will reveal the truth about Thomas Vale.”


Nobody knew what that meant.
But before midnight—
Someone made sure he would never speak again.


Your mission: Find the killer.

📁 CASE FILES

THE VICTIM

  • Name: Lord Edmund Blackwood
  • Age: 62
  • Occupation: Industrialist
  • Status: Dead

Lord Blackwood was wealthy, powerful, and feared.
Twenty-five years earlier, a factory worker named Thomas Vale died in a supposed accident at Blackwood Industrial Works.
Tonight, Lord Blackwood planned to reveal the truth.
He never got the chance.

Cause of Death

Poisoning.
The poison: Aconitine.

THE SECRET

A partially burned letter was found in the fireplace.
Only one sentence could be read:


“You took everything from me.”


Another document revealed that Lord Blackwood was planning to change his will.
His nephew, Julian, would inherit nothing.
Someone else would receive everything.

🔍 EVIDENCE

01 — THE POISONED DRINK 🥃

Aconitine was found in Lord Blackwood's brandy glass.
But the bottle itself was clean.
The poison was added after the drink was poured.

02 — THE SECOND GLASS 🍵

An empty glass was found nearby.
It contained traces of: Sugar and Lavender.
Only one suspect was known to regularly use lavender sugar.

03 — THE BROKEN WATCH ⌚

Lord Blackwood's pocket watch was found beneath his desk.
It had stopped at: 11:47 PM
The watch stopped because it struck the floor.
A struggle may have occurred.

04 — THE CLEAN KEY 🔑

The study door was locked from the inside.
The key was found on the floor.
But strangely— The key was completely clean.
The study floor was dusty.
Someone may have placed the key there after leaving the room.

05 — THE FOOTPRINT 👣

A footprint was found outside the study window.
But heavy rain had been falling for hours.
The footprint was completely dry.
It was planted.

06 — THE COAT BUTTON 🔘

A button matching Julian's coat was found beneath the desk.
But the thread attached to it was dry.
Was Julian inside the study? Or was someone trying to frame him?

  • 11:51 PM: A woman screams. Nobody admits it was them.
  • 11:58 PM — LIGHTS RETURN: The study door is locked. Lord Blackwood does not answer.
  • 12:05 AM: The door is forced open. Lord Blackwood is dead.

👥 THE SUSPECTS

SUSPECT 01 — JULIAN BLACKWOOD

Profile: Charming. Reckless. Deeply in debt. Lord Blackwood planned to remove Julian from his will.

Suspicious Facts: Publicly argued with the victim. No confirmed alibi during the blackout. His coat button was found in the study.

SUSPECT 02 — CLARA WHITMORE

Profile: Quiet. Intelligent. Returned to Ravenshire after twenty years. She once loved Lord Blackwood's missing son.

Suspicious Facts: Met privately with the victim. Left the study visibly upset. Knows about poisonous plants. Her gloves were wet.

SUSPECT 03 — DR. ADRIAN VALE

Profile: Calm. Intelligent. Trusted. But he has one secret. His father was Thomas Vale.

Suspicious Facts: Expert knowledge of poison. No confirmed alibi. Had access to the lavender sugar. Lied about his movements.

SUSPECT 04 — MRS. ELEANOR GRAVES

Profile: Worked for the family for twenty-eight years. She knows every room, every key, and every secret.

Suspicious Facts: Last confirmed person to see Lord Blackwood alive. Had access to the study and lavender sugar.
